## 1. Green Spirulina Chia Bowl with Vanilla Oat Milk

Body HTML:

```html
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>3 tbsp chia seeds</li>
  <li>1 cup vanilla-flavored oat milk</li>
  <li>½ ripe banana (mashed, for sweetness and texture)</li>
  <li>½ tsp spirulina powder (start with ¼ tsp if you’re new to the taste)</li>
  <li>1 tsp maple syrup (optional, adjust to taste)</li>
  <li>½ tsp vanilla extract (for extra flavor, optional)</li>
</ul>
<h3>Toppings</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Fresh fruits: kiwi, ground cherry, strawberries</li>
  <li>Chocolate Granola</li>
  <li>Extra splash of vanilla oat milk</li>
</ul>
<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Mix the base:</strong> In a bowl or jar, whisk together the chia seeds, vanilla oat milk, mashed banana, spirulina powder, maple syrup, and vanilla extract. Stir well to avoid clumping.</li>
  <li><strong>Let it thicken:</strong> Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (or overnight). Stir after the first 10–15 minutes to prevent the chia from settling at the bottom.</li>
  <li><strong>Assemble and top:</strong> Once set, give the pudding a good stir. Pour into a serving bowl and add your favourite toppings.</li>
  <li>Serve chilled and enjoy!</li>
</ol>
```

## 2. Creamy Mushroom Pasta

Body HTML:

```html
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Pasta of choice</li>
  <li>1 tbsp olive oil</li>
  <li>1 clove garlic, minced</li>
  <li>1 small onion, chopped</li>
  <li>1 cup mushrooms, sliced</li>
  <li>1 cup oat milk</li>
  <li>1 tbsp flour (or cornstarch for GF)</li>
  <li>Salt, pepper, thyme to taste</li>
</ul>
<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li>Cook pasta.</li>
  <li>Sauté onion, garlic, and mushrooms in oil.</li>
  <li>Sprinkle in flour or cornstarcch and cook 1 min.</li>
  <li>Slowly add oat milk while stirring.</li>
  <li>Simmer until thickened, season, and toss with pasta.</li>
</ol>
```

## 3. Strawberries & Cream Overnight Oats with Rose Syrup

Body HTML:

```html
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<h3>For the Oats:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>1 cup rolled oats</li>
  <li>1 cup unsweetened oat milk (or your milk of choice)</li>
  <li>½ cup plain or vanilla yogurt (dairy or plant-based)</li>
  <li>1 tablespoon chia seeds (for texture & thickness)</li>
  <li>1 tablespoon maple syrup or honey (optional)</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon vanilla extract</li>
  <li>Pinch of salt</li>
</ul>
<h3>For the Strawberries:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>1 cup fresh strawberries, chopped</li>
  <li>1–2 teaspoons rose syrup (adjust to taste)</li>
  <li>1 teaspoon lemon juice</li>
</ul>
<h3>Optional toppings:</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Extra strawberries or freeze-dried strawberries</li>
  <li>Crushed pistachios</li>
  <li>Drizzle of extra rose syrup or honey</li>
</ul>
<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>Mix the oats:</strong> In a bowl or jar, combine oats, oat milk, yogurt, chia seeds, vanilla, sweetener (if using), and a pinch of salt. Stir well to combine.</li>
  <li><strong>Prepare strawberries:</strong> Toss chopped strawberries with rose syrup and lemon juice. Let them macerate for about 10–15 minutes so they release their juices and absorb the floral aroma.</li>
  <li><strong>Assemble:</strong> Layer the oat mixture and strawberries in jars or containers. You can swirl them together or create layers for a pretty visual effect.</li>
  <li><strong>Chill overnight:</strong> Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).</li>
  <li><strong>Serve:</strong> In the morning, stir and top with more strawberries, a drizzle of rose syrup, and any toppings you like—nuts, petals, or yogurt dollops.</li>
</ol>
```
